Case study · Main project

AniSave

A marketplace where farmers sell straight to buyers.

https://ani-save.vercel.app/
GitHub repo ↗ Live demo ↗

The problem

Farmers often sell through middlemen and don't know what a fair price is for their crop in their own town. Buyers who want fresh produce have no direct way to reach them.

My role

Full-stack developer. I designed the MongoDB schemas, built the Express API, and made the React screens for all three users: farmer, buyer and admin.

Key features

  • Recommended pricing by municipalityFarmers see a suggested price for their area before they list a crop.
  • Income and profit estimatesEvery listing shows the expected income and profit on the stock.
  • Pre-orders and crop requestsBuyers reserve a harvest early or post a request for crops they need.
  • Admin approvals and demand viewAdmins verify accounts and see which crops are in demand.

Built with

  • MongoDB
  • Mongoose
  • Express
  • React
  • Node.js

What I learned

Building AniSave taught me to plan the data model before writing code, because changing how expenses are calculated meant reworking the schema, the API, and the screens together.

How I build

1

Map the flow

Before any code, I sketch who uses the system and what each person needs to do.

2

Model the data

I design collections and relationships in MongoDB with Mongoose schemas, so the data matches how people actually work.

3

Build the API

Express routes for every create, read, update and delete, tested one endpoint at a time until each one does exactly what it says.

4

Shape the screens

React pages that put the numbers people care about up front.
